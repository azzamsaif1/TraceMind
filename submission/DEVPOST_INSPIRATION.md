# Inspiration

Generative tools are great at making the *first* asset and terrible at governing the
thousandth. Real brand teams live in the aftermath of change: a claim gets pulled by legal,
a spokesperson's license expires, a logo is refreshed — and suddenly hundreds of live
assets are wrong, with no reliable way to find them all. We wanted the layer that sits
*after* generation and answers a single hard question: when the source changes, what media
must change with it, and can you prove it?
